TubeEarn

TubeEarn is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the YouTube channels When We Were Few (English) and Quand Nous Étions Peu (French), illustrated documentaries about prehistory.

It uploads the channel owner's own videos, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and reads the channels' own statistics. It is not offered to other users.
